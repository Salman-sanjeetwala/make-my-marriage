The Reality of Planning

A wedding has many moving parts.
Your plans deserve one place.

The guest list in a spreadsheet. Vendor details in a chat. Tasks with clear due dates and assigned responsibilities.

01 / Clarity

Plans scattered across conversations

Family WhatsApp groups move quickly. Important decisions, ceremony arrival times, and decor notes get buried under celebratory messages and voice notes.

02 / Accountability

Responsibilities that need repeated follow-ups

Without clear task ownership, loved ones either duplicate efforts on transport arrangements or assume someone else handled the dhol team arrival.

03 / Coordination

Guest responses and photos that are hard to collect

Tracking dietary notes across separate branches of the family and gathering candid photos from dozens of smartphones requires weeks of manual follow-ups.
